Earth is dead, and what's left of humanity survives in domed colonies fueled by the cerebrospinal fluid of gargantuan, half-psychic space monsters. The narrator signs on with a hunting crew and follows a captain obsessed with killing the greatest of these beasts, the legendary Mobius Beast, through the storms of Jupiter. Alexis Hall's science fiction debut turns Moby-Dick into a queer, blood-and-guts space adventure. An instant USA Today bestseller, pitched as Gideon the Ninth meets Moby-Dick.
